About This Resource

A category wayfinder and link trail built around a shared Hoobuy spreadsheet.

What this resource is

This is an independent informational and navigation resource for people who use a Hoobuy spreadsheet. It helps you locate a category, then follow a link trail from a sheet row to the current destination, the visible options, and your own decision.

What this resource is not

This resource is not the official Hoobuy website, and it is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Hoobuy or Google. It is not a store, a live link detector, a product certification tool, or a control panel.

The link trail

Everything here follows four stations: Sheet row (a discovery clue), Current destination (where the link actually opens), Visible options (what the page shows now), and Your decision (continue only when clear).

How to use it

  1. Open the shared Google Sheet to see the listing references.
  2. Browse the twelve product categories from the home page.
  3. Read the three guides before relying on any listing clue.
  4. Follow the link trail and re-check the destination yourself.
