Route Signals

Signals You Read Along the Way

These are teaching examples of what to look for — not current, live link-detection results.

  • S1

    Redirect. The link settles somewhere different from where it pointed. Note the final destination.

  • S2

    Page changed. The page’s title or layout no longer matches the clue. Re-read it.

  • S3

    Option missing. A color, size, or version is gone. Re-check what is actually selectable.

  • S4

    Link unavailable. The link does not open a usable page. Stop and locate a current one.

Stop Following Rules

When to Stop Following

  • 1

    Destination mismatch. The link opened somewhere you did not expect. Stop and re-check.

  • 2

    Option gone. A color, size, or version is missing. Stop and re-read the options.

  • 3

    Link unavailable. The page will not open. Stop and locate a current link.

  • 4

    Variant unconfirmed. Color, size, quantity, or version is still open. Stop before continuing.

  • 5

    Untraceable row. The clue cannot be traced to the Sheet or a category. Stop and find its source.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Hoobuy spreadsheet?

A Hoobuy spreadsheet is a shared document that compiles listing references, category groupings, and product links formatted for use with agent shopping services. Each row is a discovery clue, not a confirmed listing.

Is this website the official Hoobuy service?

No. This website is an independent informational and navigation resource. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Hoobuy or Google.

Does the link trail detect links or certify products?

No. The link trail is a reading framework. It does not perform live link detection, product certification, or operate as a store or control panel. Route signals are teaching examples, not current detection results.

How do I use the Hoobuy spreadsheet to find a category?

Open the shared Google Sheet and use the category links on this page to narrow to a group, then follow the link trail to confirm where a link actually opens and re-check the visible options before continuing.

What should I check before continuing from a link?

Confirm the current destination, re-check the visible options (color, size, quantity, version), and keep your decision open until anything still unconfirmed is resolved.

Where do I place an order after following a link trail?

After following the link trail and re-checking the destination page, you can continue through an agent shopping service to submit the item. This website is a navigation resource and does not process orders itself.
